Background
Google had not shipped a frontier model since Gemini 3.1 Pro more than seven months ago and skipped Gemini 3.5 entirely. Argon targets coding, enterprise knowledge work and cyber defense, and goes first to a group of trusted cyber defenders.

2 OpenAI blocks model-distillation campaign DETAIL
Background
Adversarial distillation copies a stronger model's capabilities by learning from its full reasoning traces. Researchers had shown that encrypted reasoning packets can be reused across sessions and models to decrypt them.
Why it matters
Reasoning traces are core model IP, and securing one provider's API does not protect the wider ecosystem. Defense levels differ by cloud reseller such as Azure, making security standards across the frontier-model supply chain a live issue.

>>> Defending model reasoning is only as strong as the weakest cloud channel that serves the model.

3 Google launches TPU satellite for orbital compute DETAIL
Background
Google is pursuing Project Suncatcher, a long-term plan for large compute clusters in Earth orbit. The satellite uses a Planet Labs standard platform, with a two-satellite compute-focused demo planned for next year.
Why it matters
Orbital compute has entered the experimental stage as an alternative to power- and land-constrained AI data centers. Economics hinge on launch costs falling about 20% a year, and Google's analysis implies on the order of 1,800 Starship launches.
