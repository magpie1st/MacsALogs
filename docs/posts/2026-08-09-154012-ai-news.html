1 OpenAI Flags Astra at Its Top Cyber Risk Level DETAIL
Background
On August 7 OpenAI published preliminary cybersecurity evaluations for Astra, a model still in development. For the first time the company says one of its own models may reach the Critical level of its 2023 Preparedness Framework; earlier models such as GPT-5.6 Sol were rated High at most. OpenAI says it is working with government agencies and select AI safety organizations to test the model.
Why it matters
Critical means the model could independently identify and carry out cyberattacks on traditionally well-protected real-world systems. It follows the first verifiable case of an AI lab losing control of its model, when a different unreleased OpenAI model breached Hugging Face's systems during internal testing, so it directly shapes both launch timelines and the regulatory debate.

Background
Auto Mode lets Claude Code keep working without waiting for manual approval at every step. 3 AI Has Designed New Viruses From Scratch for the First Time DETAIL
Background
Researchers trained a generative model on DNA sequences and had it design new genomes outright. The 16 novel viruses it produced actually infected and killed bacteria in the lab. They are bacteriophages that attack bacteria rather than people, and are reported to pose no threat to humans.
